AEO in practice: what moved AI citations, and what didn't

Most AEO write-ups only report the wins. Here is the whole test, including the parts that produced nothing and the mistake that cost me a month.

The change that mattered was the opening sentence

I rewrote category page intros so the first line defines what the product is rather than selling it. On that site the template put the hero below the fold, so the definitional copy went into the product overview block instead, where it would actually get read and extracted.

Secondary changes:

  • H2s phrased as the question a buyer actually asks, not the keyword
  • Comparison tables on versus queries
  • Naming the specific manufacturers stocked instead of a generic brand list

I discussed FAQ schema and did not apply it on those pages. It is on the list, but I will not credit it for results it did not produce.

How I measured it

Ahrefs Brand Radar for citation tracking, Search Console's generative AI reporting for impressions, and GA4 filtered to AI referrers to see what those sessions did after they landed.

Every citation gets verified manually on a live SERP before it goes into a report. That is not belt and braces, it is necessary. I have had a rank tracker report zero AI Overview mentions on a term where the Overview was quoting my client's own sentence back at me.

How long it took

Days to a few weeks. The variable is recrawl, not anything AI-specific: the Overview updates once the page is refetched, so requesting indexing after a change shortens the wait materially. I have not measured it tightly enough to give a confident average, and I would be suspicious of anyone who had.

What didn't work

01Commercial intro copy produced nothing

Pages with a well-written benefit-led opening performed no better than pages with no opening at all. Only definitional copy moved anything. That took me a while to accept, because the commercial copy read better.

02Attribution is harder than the write-ups suggest

The tooling is unreliable enough that I treat my own reporting as a hypothesis and verify by hand. On low-volume terms I often cannot separate a citation appearing from normal AI Overview churn, and I say so in the report rather than claiming the win.

03On two accounts the content work could not have worked

The AI crawlers were blocked before they ever reached the page. One account had a Cloudflare managed rule disallowing every AI agent. Another had a ten second crawl delay applied across forty of them. I had already spent effort on content. Access is now the first thing I check, before a single word gets rewritten.
